Cookies and preferences

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It remembers your display preferences (light or dark mode, and your language choice) in your browser's local storage. These preferences stay on your device and are not sent to us. To choose a language on your first visit, the home page checks your browser's language and time-zone settings on your device; that check also happens only in your browser. You can remove these preferences at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.
