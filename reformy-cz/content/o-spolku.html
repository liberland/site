Who we are

An independent Czech association

Reformy.cz z.s. is a nonprofit organisation entered in the register of associations kept by the Municipal Court in Prague. We are based in Hradčany, Prague 1, and work in economic education, public debate and the dissemination of the ideas of a free society.

The association builds on the Klub Reformy.cz, founded in 2011 as an open platform for debate on economic and social reform. Since then we have run the reformy.cz website, where we publish commentary, analysis and educational material.

We are a separate legal entity under Czech law. We operate as an association, do not distribute profit and carry out all our activities in line with our registered purpose. We are not a political party or a state body and do not act on behalf of any state.

Our mission

Understanding economics means understanding freedom

“The purpose of the association is education in the field of economics and the dissemination of the ideas of freedom and the market economy.”

Registered purpose of the association, public register · Czech original: „Účelem spolku je vzdělávání v oblasti ekonomie a šíření myšlenek svobody a tržního hospodářství.“

We believe people who understand how value is created, how prices work and what public decisions lead to are better able to make free and informed choices. That is why we explain economics in plain language and open up debate on topics that are rarely discussed elsewhere.

  • Economic literacy

    Everyone should understand basic concepts such as prices, savings, taxes and public debt. Without them, no one can make informed decisions about public affairs.

  • Personal freedom

    Everyone has the right to run their own life, as long as they do not restrict the same right of others.

  • Personal responsibility

    Freedom and responsibility belong together. Whoever makes a decision also bears its consequences.

  • Voluntary cooperation

    The most lasting results come from cooperation that people enter into voluntarily and on fair terms.

  • Open debate

    Opinions should be tested by argument. We give room to views that are not held by the majority.

  • Market economy

    Free exchange, protection of property and competition are the best known way to raise living standards.

Ongoing

Publishing and education

We run the reformy.cz website. We publish commentary and analysis on economics, energy, European policy and public finance, and educational material for the general public.

  • original articles and commentary on reformy.cz
  • the Education section: films, quotes, recommended books

In preparation

We organise and support debates, lectures and meetings where people can discuss economics and freedom in person. We build on the Klub Reformy.cz conference of January 2011.

Reformy.cz and Liberland

Two separate entities, one shared interest

Liberland is an international initiative and community working towards a society built on personal freedom, voluntary action and a minimal role for the state. Reformy.cz z.s. has cooperated with this community for a long time because its aims match our registered purpose: education in economics and the dissemination of the ideas of freedom and the market economy.

The cooperation works both ways. The association provides services and support to the Liberland community, including its governing structures. In return, the community enables the association to pursue its educational and outreach mission internationally.

What the association does for the community

  • organisational and administrative services
  • membership- and community-related services
  • management of the communication infrastructure needed for the cooperation
  • participation in international outreach and community activities

What the community brings to the association

  • an international audience for educational content
  • partners for debates, lectures and meetings
  • practical examples of the voluntary institutions we teach about
